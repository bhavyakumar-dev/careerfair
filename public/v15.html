14 / SOUND STUDIO

Make the sounds
work for you.

Explore a few high-impact English sound contrasts with a visual mouth-placement guide and optional model audio. The aim is intelligibility—not copying somebody else's accent.

MOUTH PLACEMENT
Illustrative guide · not a medical model

/θ/

TH as in think

Let the tongue tip appear just between the teeth and send a small stream of air through the gap. Keep the sound unvoiced.

Keep your jaw relaxed. Do not clamp your teeth together or force the tongue forward.

thinkthreethoughthealthy

Try: think / sink · three / tree

English pronunciation varies across regions and speakers. No accent is automatically better; work toward being understood by the people you need to communicate with.

YOUR INFORMATION

Practice should
feel safe.

Read this before uploading a recording or joining the shared leaderboard. You decide what to share, and you should never upload another person’s voice or video without permission.

01

Audio and video

Newsroom videos are uploaded to the project’s private storage only after you tick the consent box and select Analyze video & save. The video is stored so the project team can review or delete it. Audio/video analysis is sent to the AI providers configured by the administrator. Do not include sensitive details or other people without consent.

02

Leaderboard permission

Joining the shared board is optional and requires a separate checkbox. Use a nickname. Your nickname, class and practice scores are visible to other visitors if you join. Ask the administrator to remove your entry if you change your mind.

03

Feedback and support

Feedback and support requests are saved for the project team when server storage is configured. An optional email is only needed if you want a reply. Do not submit passwords, API keys or sensitive personal information.

04

What the scores mean

Scores are practice signals, not an official exam, intelligence measure or guarantee of admission or employment. Video feedback focuses on visible presentation elements such as framing, posture and gesture—not on inferring personality or other personal traits.

For a school event, ask the school and a parent or guardian to approve server-side recording, AI processing, storage duration and leaderboard publication before students use these features.
