Act III: The Colleges

Five Indian institutions with strong English programs. Scroll sideways through the company. Each college card includes images and detailed information.

Delhi

St. Stephen's College

University of Delhi

Widely recognised as one of India's top institutions for English Studies. Known for a rigorous critical theory curriculum, vibrant literary societies, and graduates in academia, civil services and journalism.

  • 🎓 BA/MA in English Literature
  • 📚 Specialization: Literary Theory & Criticism
  • 👥 Notable Alumni: Civil Service Officers, Journalists
  • 🌟 Heritage: Established 1881

Theory-Focused • Co-ed • Urban Delhi

Mumbai

St. Xavier's College

Autonomous Institution

A historic premier institute for humanities and liberal arts, covering literary history, contemporary postcolonial literature, creative writing and media studies. Strong industry connections in media and publishing.

  • 🎓 BA/MA in English (Autonomous)
  • 📚 Specialization: Creative Writing & Media
  • 👥 Career Path: Publishers, Content Creators
  • 🌟 Accreditation: NAAC A+

Practical • Co-ed • Urban Mumbai

Kolkata

Presidency University

Central University

Formerly Presidency College, with a legacy in Indian higher education. Its English department emphasises literary criticism, comparative literature and cultural analysis. Kolkata's literary heritage permeates the curriculum.

  • 🎓 BA/MA in English (Central University)
  • 📚 Specialization: Indian & Comparative Lit
  • 👥 Alumni: Renowned Writers & Academics
  • 🌟 Heritage: Oldest institution (1817)

Theory-Focused • Co-ed • Urban Kolkata

Delhi

Lady Shri Ram College

University of Delhi (Women)

A leading liberal arts college for women in South Asia, focused on feminist literary theory, world literature, rhetoric and modern literary studies. Strong student publication culture and debate societies.

  • 🎓 BA/MA in English (Women's College)
  • 📚 Specialization: Feminist Theory & Rhetoric
  • 👥 Empowerment: Leadership in Media & Policy
  • 🌟 Strong: Debate & Literary Clubs

Theory-Focused • Women's • Urban Delhi

Hyderabad

EFLU (English & Foreign Languages University)

Central University

The only Central University devoted exclusively to English and foreign languages. Offers degrees in literature, linguistics, English Language Teaching (ELT), and media and communication. Cutting-edge focus on technology and linguistics.

  • 🎓 BA/MA in English (Central University)
  • 📚 Specialization: Linguistics, ELT, Media
  • 👥 Career Path: ELT Professionals, Linguists
  • 🌟 Unique: Tech + Language Integration

Practical • Co-ed • University Town
