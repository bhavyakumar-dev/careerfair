Design a polished, responsive English-and-career exploration website called “Open Mic — English for the work you want” for Class X students and parents at a school career fair. Use a cinematic dark hero with colourful, editorial career sections, custom abstract 3D-inspired shapes, confident typography and thoughtful micro-interactions. Make the experience one continuous scroll with no bottom navigation. Include sections for career discovery, a short career quiz and roadmap, speaking practice, mock interviews, conversation roleplay, news-anchor teleprompter, communication comparison, great speakers, an opt-in leaderboard, vocabulary, an email-writing game and a share QR. Keep the experience accessible, mobile-first and calm rather than a generic AI dashboard. Admin controls should be able to switch themes and hide individual sections cleanly.
